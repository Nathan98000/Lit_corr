# name: hedges
# label: Hedging words
# description: Words and phrases that soften, qualify or make a statement less definite ("maybe", "seemed", "sort of").
# notes: "could" is excluded (it mostly expresses ability in past-tense narration: "she could feel"); "kind of"/"sort of" are excluded (mostly "the kind of man"), the informal "kinda"/"sorta" are kept. "might" is also counted as a modal verb (a separate measure). "a little" alone is excluded because it is mostly about size or amount.
