# notes: Added in Stage 1B. Language, not depicted consent: a check-in can be ignored in the story, and refusals ("stop", "no") are not counted. Generic questions ("do you want to", "are you sure", "do you want me to") were 48% of hits in the Stage 1B prototype and mostly not about sex, so they are left out, as are "check in" (hotels) and "tell me if" (generic). Essays that discuss consent also score high. A Stage 1B hold-out hand-check found taunts ("is this what you want", "too much for you", "do you want this") and generic requests ("is it alright") among the hits, so those entries are left out too; the idiom "better to ask forgiveness than permission" is excluded.
